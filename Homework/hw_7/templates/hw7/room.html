HW7 Part 3 · Room Dimensions from Video

Recovers the camera path and a sparse 3D reconstruction, finds the room's corners and its floor and ceiling lines on its own, and fixes the real-world scale from a known measurement. Assumes a rectangular room.

Method

Key frames are matched with SIFT. An essential matrix on the pair with the best parallax starts the reconstruction, then each further frame is registered by PnP and new points are triangulated. This gives a pose \(P_i = K[R_i\,|\,t_i]\) per frame, but only up to an unknown scale \(s\).

Plain walls have few features, so the room is read from its straight edges. Where the floor line stops at the same 3D point across frames, that point is a corner, triangulated through the cameras. One real measurement fixes the scale:

$$s = \frac{\text{known length (mm)}}{\text{same length in the reconstruction}}$$

In one frame the floor and ceiling lines are parallel in 3D, and both lie on the wall at distance \(Z\). With \(n\) the normal of a line's back-projected plane, and \(w\), \(u\) the wall normal and up, each line sits at \(y = -Z\,(n\cdot w)/(n\cdot u)\), so the height is the difference between the two, taken as the median over frames.

Video stabilisation crops the frame, so the focal length is corrected from the room itself: the front and side walls are square, so their vanishing points satisfy \((v_1-p)\cdot(v_2-p)+f^2=0\). Width is measured only if both ends of the front wall appear. Any other known measurement you enter is reported as an independent check.

Capture

  • Use one video, moving the camera sideways from left to right along the front wall. Turning on the spot gives no depth.
  • The front wall is the wall the camera faces. Upload the video and enter the camera-to-front-wall distance: nothing needs marking.
  • Width needs both the left and right corners of the front wall in the video, and height needs the ceiling line and the floor line.
  • Length uses back-wall corners if you mark them. If not, it uses the camera-to-front-wall distance, which assumes the camera was at the back wall.
