HW7 · Stereo Measurement & Room Dimensions

Real-world sizes from calibrated stereo geometry and from video reconstruction.

Part 1 · Calibrated stereo object measurement

Upload a left and a right photo with the calibration and baseline. The app rectifies the pair, computes disparity and depth, and turns the points you select into 3D coordinates. It measures rectangles, circles and polygons.

Part 2 · Uncalibrated stereo

Handwritten derivation, submitted separately.

Part 3 · Room dimensions from video

Upload one video that moves sideways along the room. The app extracts key frames, recovers the camera path and a sparse 3D reconstruction, then finds the room's corners and its floor and ceiling lines on its own. A known measurement sets the real scale.
